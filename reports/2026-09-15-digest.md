## Main list

**1. Atria Dawn: The Dawn of Agentic Superintelligence** · 4/5  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15818) · [Code](https://github.com/atria-asi/Atria-Dawn-Preview) · [Project](https://atria-asi.ai)  
A foundation agentic LLM trained via a Verifiable Experience Pipeline that connects tool use to executable environments with externally verified outcomes. Claims strong results across 16 benchmarks spanning research, engineering, and digital tasks. 363 HF upvotes on day one signals immediate community interest. The framing — agents as participants in producing their successors — is the clearest articulation yet of recursive self-improvement as a near-term engineering target.

**2. SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models** · 4/5  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886) · [Code](https://github.com/Junchao-cs/SolarWM) · [Project](https://junchao-cs.github.io/SolarWM-Web/)  
Continuing story (10th run, first seen 2026-09-03). The most complete open stack for interactive video world models: unified data prep across heterogeneous sources, training recipes for multiple video backbones, and long-horizon inference. 645 GitHub stars and 152 HF upvotes — traction keeps compounding. If you need a world-model foundation to build on, this is the reference.

**3. nvidia/PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles** · 4/5  
[Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles)  
NVIDIA's autonomous-vehicle dataset drop — multi-sensor, licensed for research. Second radar appearance, traction flat. Valuable primarily as a standardized benchmark substrate for Physical AI work; the real signal is NVIDIA committing to open data for robotics.

**4. GAIA Benchmark** · 4/5  
[Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gaia-benchmark/GAIA)  
The general AI assistant benchmark (multimodal, tool-use, reasoning) — 5th run, traction steady. Remains the go-to eval for agentic breadth. If you're building agents, you're measuring against GAIA.

**5. TokenRhythm/NeoHorse-1-4B** · 3/5  
[Model](https://huggingface.co/TokenRhythm/NeoHorse-1-4B)  
4B agentic model (Qwen3.5 backbone) optimized for tool use and routing. Released alongside the NeoHorse-1 paper (arXiv:2609.08183) which is on a 5-run story arc (+64% traction). Small, deployable, and explicitly built for the agentic post-training loop.

**6. ukisai/Swift-Qwen3.8-27b** · 3/5  
[Model](https://huggingface.co/ukisai/Swift-Qwen3.8-27b)  
27B efficient-thinking variant with image-text-to-text capability. Qwen3.8 base, safetensors. Trending on HF but no public benchmarks or repo yet — treat as a capability probe, not a drop-in replacement.

## Insights

- **Recursive self-improvement has moved from theory to engineering sprint.** Atria Dawn, NeoHorse, Dream-RSI, and RSIAgent all appeared in the same 48h window — each with a different angle (verifiable experience, routing harness, evolving worlds, autonomous exploration) but the same loop: agent → environment → verified outcome → training signal.
- **Video world models are consolidating around SolarWM as the open foundation.** 10 runs, 3.5× traction growth, and now NVIDIA's PhysicalAI dataset landing in the same cluster. The stack is maturing: data → training → long-horizon inference, all open.
- **Agentic benchmarks are the new model cards.** GAIA (5th run), BVB (new), and the 16-benchmark suite in Atria Dawn — papers now lead with eval breadth, not just loss curves. If you're not measuring on these, you're not in the conversation.
- **Small agentic models are shipping before large ones.** NeoHorse-1-4B and Swift-Qwen3.8-27b are deployable today; the 70B+ agentic foundations are still in preview. The adoption curve will run bottom-up.

## Action items

- **Read** Atria Dawn paper + NeoHorse-1 paper (arXiv:2609.08183) together — they're the clearest paired view of where agentic post-training is headed.
- **Try** SolarWM's training stack if you need video world-model infrastructure; the repo is production-grade and the data pipeline handles heterogeneity that breaks naive approaches.
- **Track** the Recursive Self-Improvement cluster (Dream-RSI, RSIAgent, NeoHorse, Atria Dawn) — one of these will likely produce the first credible "agent improves agent" demo within 3 months.
- **Benchmark** your agents on GAIA and BVB when it lands; they're becoming the de facto standard for agentic breadth and video understanding respectively.
