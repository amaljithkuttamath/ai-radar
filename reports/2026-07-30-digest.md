## Main list

- [Parallel Decoding Distillation for Fast Image and Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26004) · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/fastgen) · [project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/genair/pdd/)  
  Nvidia proposes a new method for rapid image and video generation, distilling iterative diffusion models into efficient parallel decoders. The repo is live—894 stars in two days—showcasing ~4x faster generation with minimal quality loss. This is a real SOTA acceleration, not just a tweak. A must-read for anyone doing generative media. · 4/5

- [ReDesign: Recovering Editable Design Structures from Images via Agentic Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25565) · [code](https://github.com/jintae-00/ReDesign) · [project](https://jintae-00.github.io/ReDesign/)  
  Tackles the notorious “raster-to-vector” workflow bottleneck in design automation: recovering editable and layered design files from a single image. The agentic approach and usable code (58 HF upvotes, 44 GitHub stars) make this a practical jump in productivity for designers and ML engineers alike. · 4/5

- [Dokploy/dokploy — Open Source Alternative to Vercel, Netlify and Heroku.](https://github.com/Dokploy/dokploy)  
  An open-source deployment platform gaining serious traction—36,205 GitHub stars in 48h—positioning itself as a viable alternative to incumbent cloud tools. Developers looking for self-hosted, cost-effective deployment now have a mainstream option. · 3/5

- [XYZAILab/XYZ-Aquila-SFT (HF Dataset)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/XYZAILab/XYZ-Aquila-SFT)  
  New supervised fine-tuning dataset for English and Chinese text generation and QA, trending on Hugging Face. Diversity in language and task categories signals value for training multi-lingual or multi-task models. · 3/5

- [UrbanDS: A Graph-Guided LLM Multi-Agent System for Data-Intensive Urban Tasks](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26724v1)  
  Automated data science for urban analytics via graph-guided multi-agent LLMs. Concrete results and a usable artifact, targeting scaling and practical impact—of interest to those building agentic data platforms or city intelligence tools. · 3/5
