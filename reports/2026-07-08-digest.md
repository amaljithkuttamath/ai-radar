  Introduces DataGovBench, derived from real-world government data. Benchmarks LLM performance on complex datasets—multi-tabular, external knowledge, and exploratory analysis—addressing limitations of factoid table benchmarks. Artifact included. · 3/5

- [VendorBench-100: A Unified Cross-Paradigm Benchmark for Deepfake Image Detection](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06254v1)

- Robotics and simulated environments are trending toward richer, multi-modal and dynamic representations (RGB-DF, playable video worlds).

- Open-source agent platforms (FinRobot) are seeing explosive traction, indicating strong demand for actionable, tool-centric LLM solutions in verticals like finance.
- Industry classification and mapping artifacts (NAICS-GH) directly tie LLM advances to sectoral and innovation geography analysis.
