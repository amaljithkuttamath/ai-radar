## Main list

### LynnReal-Omni: Native multi-modal Video Generation for Agentic Visual Workflows · 4/5  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15863) · [Code](https://github.com/LynnReal-AI/LynnReal-Omni) · [Project](https://github.com/LynnReal-AI/LynnReal-Omni)  
A diffusion transformer system that generates video conditioned on explicit 3D scene references, editable game states, and executable agent plans — moving beyond prompt-only control toward verifiable visual workflows. The repo has 204 stars and 53 HF upvotes; traction has nearly quadrupled since September 15. This is the only carryover item still climbing hard enough to warrant a main-list slot again.

### camel-ai/oasis — OASIS: Open Agent Social Interaction Simulations with One Million Agents · 3/5  
[GitHub](https://github.com/camel-ai/oasis)  
A framework for running million-agent social simulations with open-ended interaction protocols. 5,171 GitHub stars in days signals serious community pull. The scale is the headline: most agent benches top out at dozens; this pushes to population-level dynamics with observable emergent behaviors (deception, coalition formation, market pricing). Worth tracking for multi-agent eval infrastructure.

### AlexWortega/openjev · 3/5  
[HF Model](https://huggingface.co/AlexWortega/openjev)  
A Qwen3.5-based cross-encoder reranker trained for NLI and retrieval. No public benchmarks or code repo surfaced yet — the signal is HF trending momentum from a known contributor. Park here until a reproducible eval or integration appears.

### Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1 · 3/5  
[HF Model](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1)  
Qwen's latest text-to-image model adding RGBA output for transparent-layer editing and improved instruction-following for image editing. Weights on HF, diffusers integration ready. Incremental on architecture but the editing affordances are practical for design-to-code workflows.

## Insights

- **Agentic simulation infrastructure is outpacing model releases.** OASIS (5.1k stars) and LynnReal-Omni (287% traction growth) both center on *environments where agents act*, not just better single-model weights. The community is voting for eval harnesses and controllable generation over raw capability bumps.
- **Recursive self-improvement papers are forming a durable cluster.** RSIAgent, ScienceBuddy, Miles, and the self-distillation paper (privileged information) have all sustained multi-run traction — a rare signal coherence for a pre-paradigm topic.
- **Dataset releases from major labs (NVIDIA, GAIA) show high initial scores but flat long-tail traction.** They enter the radar on brand + artifact quality, then stall unless a downstream breakthrough consumes them.
