## Main list

### 1. SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models · 4/5
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886) · [Code](https://github.com/Junchao-cs/SolarWM) · [Project](https://junchao-cs.github.io/SolarWM-Web/)  
A fully open pipeline — data curation, training recipes, and checkpoints — for video world models that handle heterogeneous sources (egocentric, driving, robotics) with varying camera geometries and motion statistics. The 362 → 1,118 star surge in 48 hours signals the community treating this as the de facto starter kit. **Continuing story** — 2nd run, traction still accelerating.

### 2. Out-of-Distribution Generalisation with Sequence Models in Offline Multi-Agent RL · 3/5
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03667v1)  
Extends offline sequence modeling to variable agent counts and task spaces; finds **task diversity beats dataset size** for zero-shot transfer. Code and multi-task datasets released. Relevant if you're building multi-agent systems that must generalize beyond training tasks.

### 3. Beyond Straightness: Non-Crossing Flow Matching via Quantile AlignTree Coupling · 3/5
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03443v1)  
QAT-FM builds hierarchical couplings in O(Nd log N) — avoiding path crossings of independent coupling and the cost of OT. Beats iCFM and OT-FM on image benchmarks with 3–5× faster coupling construction. Usable artifact linked.

### 4. Enhancing Financial QA: FinRAG-QA Benchmark · 3/5
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03654v1)  
999 practitioner-curated questions over 209 annual/Pillar-3 reports from 24 major banks (2019–2023). Targets the inhomogeneity of cross-jurisdiction financial tables — a genuine gap in current RAG benchmarks. Dataset and evaluation code released.

### 5. NeoRed: MLLM for Neonatal Respiratory Diagnosis · 3/5
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03527v1)  
First MLLM trained on neonatal chest X-rays (NeoCXR/NeoCXR-EV datasets). Aligns visual, clinical-text, and logic reasoning modules; outperforms generalist MLLMs on diagnosis accuracy. Medical-domain specialists: the dataset alone is valuable.

### 6. FrameBench: Language Understanding via Frame Semantics · 3/5
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03370v1)  
Tests whether LLMs implicitly enrich text with unstated frame-semantic knowledge (e.g., "John bought a car" → seller, price, transfer of possession). Multiple-choice tasks derived from FrameNet. A clean probe for world-model-style reasoning.

### 7. EraseSAE: Surgical Concept Erasure in Text-to-Video via Sparse Autoencoders · 3/5
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03629v1)  
Uses SAEs to isolate and ablate single concepts in T2V diffusion models (copyrighted characters, unsafe content) without degrading unrelated generations. Code released. Directly applicable if you're shipping T2V and need fine-grained safety controls.

### 8. yuxin-jiang/Anomagic — Crossmodal Zero-Shot Anomaly Generation · 3/5
[GitHub](https://github.com/yuxin-jiang/Anomagic) (158★)  
AAAI 2026 implementation: generates anomalies from text prompts across image/video/audio without anomaly training data. 158 stars in 48h from GitHub Trending. Useful for synthetic anomaly data augmentation.

### 9. openai-community/gpt2 (HF Model) · 3/5
[Model](https://huggingface.co/openai-community/gpt2)  
Trending on HF again — likely driven by quantization/benchmarking workflows using it as a baseline. Not new science, but a signal that lightweight baselines remain workload-relevant.

### 10. google-bert/bert-base-uncased (HF Model) · 3/5
[Model](https://huggingface.co/google-bert/bert-base-uncased)  
Same pattern as GPT-2: resurgent as a cheap encoder baseline for distillation, retrieval, and quantization experiments.
