### Planning
**GameHorizon Suite: Multi-Horizon Data and Evaluation in Gameplay** · 4/5  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25001) · [Code](https://github.com/TencentARC/GameHorizon) · [Project](https://gamehorizon-suite.github.io/) — A unified benchmark suite spanning 100+ games with language instructions, replacing high-variance online rollouts with fixed multi-horizon evaluation. 259 GitHub stars, 130 HF upvotes. 4th run, traction still rising (Δ+3.08× since Sep 22). Continuing story, not a new arrival.

**GRASP: Generating, Revising, and Assessing for Strategic Planning with Agentic AI** · 3/5  
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30147v1) — Three-stage planning pipeline (GenPlan → RevPlan → AssessPlan) that isolates context per stage to prevent compounding errors on complex tasks. Artifact released. Strong-group authorship.

**Agentic Detection of Online Conspiracies** · 3/5  
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30250v1) — Frames conspiracy detection as intent inference via social-context tools (profile history, network position, reply graphs) rather than claim matching. Artifact released. Strong-group authorship.

**Augur: A Synthetic Decision Lab for Rehearsing Reactions to Product and Policy Changes** · 3/5  
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29952v1) — Builds a typed knowledge graph from change docs, simulates a grounded persona market, and emits a five-way release verdict. Tested on Gold-50 real episodes with known outcomes; finds most signal comes from structure, not LLM sophistication. Artifact released.

## Insights
- **Planning is fragmenting into specialized stages** — GRASP's GenPlan/RevPlan/AssessPlan split, Augur's graph→persona→simulate→memo pipeline, and GameHorizon's multi-horizon eval all point to "one big context" being the wrong abstraction for complex agency.
- **Evaluation is eating methodology** — Three of the top items (GameHorizon, Augur, SAGE) contribute primarily *measurement infrastructure*; the field is investing in better rulers before better models.
- **Synthetic data pipelines are moving upstream** — SciWalker and XiaomiMiMo both target the *generation* of verifiable training tasks, not just model training on fixed sets.
- **SVG as a reasoning substrate** — SVGLM and LynnReal-Omni independently converge on vector graphics as the editable, differentiable bridge between vision and language reasoning.
