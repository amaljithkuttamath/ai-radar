### Cost

**NVIDIA Vera Rubin Maximizes Intelligence per Dollar for Post-Training Workloads — a Key Metric for Agentic AI**  
[NVIDIA claims Vera Rubin hardware achieves lowest cost per token](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-vera-rubin-post-training-intelligence-per-dollar/) for post-training, targeting future "agentic" AI workloads. Structured for executive decision-making, this initiative aims to bring standardized productivity metrics to large-scale AI deployments.  
Score: 1/5 (watch-list; relevant for practitioners tracking ROI)
