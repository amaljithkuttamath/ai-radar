## Main list

  A production-grade toolkit from NVIDIA for prepping and curating LLM training data at scale. Usable out of the box, and rapidly accumulating traction—1,659 GitHub stars. This is a robust, industry-standard solution for anyone tackling large-scale LLM pipeline challenges. · 4/5

- [taichengguo/LLM_MultiAgents_Survey_Papers — Large Language Model based Multi-Agents: A Survey of Progress and Challenges (In IJCAI 2024)](https://github.com/taichengguo/LLM_MultiAgents_Survey_Papers)

- [rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch — Implement a ChatGPT-like LLM in PyTorch from scratch, step by step](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch)  
  A hands-on, pedagogical repo walking through implementation and training of a ChatGPT-like LLM in PyTorch. Extremely popular: nearly 100,000 GitHub stars. Ideal for learners, educators, and prototyping teams wanting open, granular insight into modern LLMs. · 3/5

- [paperless-ngx/paperless-ngx — A community-supported supercharged document management system: scan, index and archive all your documents](https://github.com/paperless-ngx/paperless-ngx)
