### Benchmark

**[Beyond Measurement Metrics: A Human-Centered Framework for Semantic Validation of Network Traffic Classification](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17014v1) · 3/5**  
Reframes network-traffic classification evaluation around semantic validity — does the model learn *meaningful* patterns or spurious correlations? Adapts a knowledge-generation framework with a human-centered validation layer. Code and data released. Relevant if you build classifiers that must justify decisions, not just maximize AUC.

**[World Models for Embodied Intelligence: From Plausible to Controllable to Actionable](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16697v1) · 3/5**  
Survey arguing that world-model progress should be measured by behavioral improvement (anticipating weight, resistance, contact) not visual fidelity. Maps predictive capabilities to control outcomes. Strong framing for anyone building or evaluating simulators for robotics.

**[ParsHate: A Benchmark Dataset for Hate and Target Detection in Persian](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16393v1) · 3/5**  
First decade-scale (2013–2022) Persian hate-speech benchmark: 10k manually annotated tweets, 31% hateful, seven target categories, explicit/implicit distinction, span-level rationales. Stratifed temporal sampling reduces keyword bias. Dataset and evaluation scripts released. Fills a real low-resource gap.

**[Skeletal Prototypes on Iterative Nerve Expansions (SPINE)](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16170v1) · 2/5**  
Prototype reduction where each class is an embedded 1-complex (graph) rather than a point set. Initial edges from a class-conditional Mapper graph; vertices fitted under classification objective. Novel geometric take on condensation — watch for follow-up on larger scales.

### Cost

**[Coverage-Aware Virtual IMU Augmentation for Low-Resource Human Activity Recognition](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16768v1) · 3/5**  
Generates synthetic IMU samples only where they expand coverage of the sensor manifold, avoiding redundant or misleading augmentation. Shows gains on public HAR benchmarks with few labeled subjects. Code released. Practical for wearable/edge teams data-constrained in deployment.

**[Smarter by the Moment: Environment-Driven Dynamic Policies for Continual LLM Improvement](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16800v1) · 3/5**  
DRPG framework: retrieves past episodes, synthesizes *policies* (not just examples), and updates them via environment feedback. Reduces repeated error patterns on continual benchmarks. Artifact available. Interesting for LLM ops teams seeking cheaper adaptation than full retraining.
