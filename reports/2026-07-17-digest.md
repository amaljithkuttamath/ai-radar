# AI Radar — 2026-07-17

A sharp surge this window in open and efficient agent systems, benchmarking, and multimodal models — notably practical advances in video understanding and agent evaluation. Broad traction for a curated world model paper collection (266 GitHub stars) and real artifact drops signal concrete progress in both technical depth and usability. Methods for driving down inference cost and extending access to older hardware also feature prominently.

## Main list

### Agents
- [SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14777) · [code](https://github.com/jinyangwu/SEED) · [project](https://jinyangwu.github.io/seed/)  
  RL distillation method for agent training, improving efficiency on long-horizon tasks with sparse rewards; 55 HF upvotes signal strong traction. Artifact is available. · 3/5

- [StructureClaw: Traceable LLM Agents and an Executable Benchmark for Structural Engineering Workflows](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14896v1)  
  Introduces a benchmark that captures full evidence chains for agentic workflows in engineering — beyond simple Q&A or script generation. Real-world context and artifact, grounded in traceable outputs. · 3/5

- [OmniaBench: Benchmarking General AI Agents Across Diverse Scenarios](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14989v1)  
  New benchmark suite targeting broad evaluation of agentic LLMs, covering diverse interaction and tool-use scenarios. Artifact and protocol are public. · 3/5

- [LIGO-PINN: Learned Initialization via Gated Optimization to Alleviate Convergence Failures in Physics Informed Neural Networks](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14233v1)  
  Tackles convergence failures in PINNs using learned gated optimization — of interest for agent workflows in complex environments. Artifact available; the method challenges previous limitations in physics-driven RL. · 3/5

### Cost
- [VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14935) · [github](https://github.com/MCG-NJU/VideoChat3) · [project](https://mcg-nju.github.io/VideoChat3)  
  Fully open, highly efficient video MLLM pushing generalist video understanding, with 84 HF upvotes and a public repo. Designed for real-world, low-cost inference across diverse video types; relevant for practical deployment. · 4/5

- [Stop Thinking, Start Looking: Efficient Post-Training for Multimodal Document Question Answering via Reasoning-Free Alignment](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14682v1)  
  Efficient method for document QA that skips costly reasoning steps by aligning answers directly to visual evidence, improving speed and reducing resource demands. Usable artifact released. · 3/5

- High-signal benchmarks and curated corpora are gaining traction, driving clarity and reproducibility in agent evaluation and world modeling.
