## Main list

### Moe
- [LuffyTheFox/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-Uncensored-Genesis-Hermes-V6-GGUF (HF Model)](https://huggingface.co/LuffyTheFox/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-Uncensored-Genesis-Hermes-V6-GGUF)  
  A new Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) large language model covering image-to-text and vision tasks, tagged uncensored and “Genesis Hermes”. Now surfaced in trending, with real traction among practitioners. · 3/5

- [microsoft/Fara1.5-27B (HF Model)](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Fara1.5-27B)  
  Microsoft’s 27B-parameter image-text-to-text foundation model continues to gather attention, now tracked for a second run. Tags indicate compatibility with Qwen and safetensors for deployment flexibility. · 3/5

### Benchmark
- [Correr-Zhou/OmniShow — [ICML 2026] ByteDance's All-in-One Video Generation Model for Human-Object Interaction Video Generation](https://github.com/Correr-Zhou/OmniShow)  
  ByteDance's entry for ICML 2026 tackles comprehensive video generation, focused on human-object interaction—trending strongly on GitHub (463 stars). Usable repo, likely to set baseline for new multimodal benchmarks. · 3/5

- [OmniPhys: Knowledge-Graph-Driven Benchmarking and Collective Optimization for Physical Commonsense in Text-to-Image Generation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25641v1)  
  Advances benchmarking for “commonsense” constraints in image generation using knowledge graphs to evaluate text-to-image models’ mastery of physical principles—usable artifact, frontier group. · 3/5

### Other
- [HiFi-UMI: Learning Deployable Manipulation Policies from High-Fidelity UMI Data Alone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25895) · [project](https://cloud.simpleai.tech/simple-world-lab/hifi-umi/)  
  Frontier robotics team shows manipulation policies trained entirely on high-fidelity UMI data, bypassing real-robot data bottlenecks. Trending with strong signals (86 HF upvotes), concrete artifact ready for deployment. · 4/5

- [ReDesign: Recovering Editable Design Structures from Images via Agentic Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25565) · [code](https://github.com/jintae-00/ReDesign) · [project](https://jintae-00.github.io/ReDesign/)  
  Extracts structured, editable design files from raster images using agentic decomposition and multi-modal reasoning—practical solution for design teams, cited by strong-group authors, repo live. · 4/5

- [MyoCardBench: A Real-World Data Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models in Clinically Authentic Cardiovascular Care Scenarios](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25186v1)  
  Expands benchmarking beyond textbook medicine to real longitudinal and safety-critical clinical workflows, especially in cardiovascular care—not just simulated QA, but authentic operational scenarios. Artifact available. · 3/5

- [An Embarrassingly Simple Rule-based Visiting Circulation Approach to Trip Destination Prediction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25751v1)  
  Achieves competitive trip prediction using a rule-based model—results represent a practical baseline for urban mobility forecasting, usable artifact, strong group. · 3/5

- [TRWH: A Text-Driven Random Walk Heterogeneous GNN for Semantic-Aware Sparse Recommendation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25471v1)  
  Integrates GNNs and LLMs for semantic-aware recommendations on sparse graphs, targeting recommendation systems where structure and meaning must both be captured; practical artifact, not just proposal. · 3/5

- [AMRD: Adaptive Multi-Teacher Relational Distillation for Lightweight Speech Emotion Recognition](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25289v1)  
  Compresses large speech models for real-time edge deployment using adaptive, multi-teacher distillation. Reliable speech emotion recognition now feasible on devices. Usable artifact, strong-group authors. · 3/5

## Insights

- Agentic distillation and practical benchmarks are moving quickly: nearly all surfaced items offer usable artifacts.
- Medical and urban mobility benchmarking are broadening beyond simulated tasks—real-world, longitudinal data is increasingly the bar.
- MoE models and video generation systems hold practitioner attention, especially those landing artifacts with live traction.
- Rule-based and hybrid approaches are resurging, setting new practical baselines as complexity sometimes yields to clarity.
