## Main List

### Benchmark

- [Accurate, Interdisciplinary and Transparent Structure-property Understanding with Deep Native Structural Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07708) · [github](https://github.com/SpectrAI-Initiative/SciReasoner) · [project](https://scireasoner.github.io)  
  A high-signal advance in scientific AI: this system blends transparent structural reasoning with machine learning for structure-property predictions in biology, chemistry, and materials physics. The artifact is live (repo + project site), and traction is strong (65 HF upvotes), marking it as a benchmark for explainable interdisciplinary modeling. · 4/5

- [MIRA-Math: A Benchmark for Minimal Information Requesting and Mathematical Reasoning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07391v1)  
  MIRA-Math pioneers a diagnostic benchmark for evaluating how models solve math problems when only minimal information can be requested, stressing reasoning over tool-use or retrieval. The focus is unique: the benchmark enforces disciplined problem-state analysis, and artifacts are available. · 3/5

- [Where to Intervene? Benchmarking Fairness-Aware Learning on Differentially Private Synthetic Tabular Data](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07471v1)  
  A timely benchmark: this work interrogates the tension between privacy (via Differential Privacy) and fairness in synthetic tabular datasets, clarifying how interventions change both properties. Artifact and empirical results are provided. · 3/5

- [Enhancing deep learning models for time series classification via knowledge distillation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06796v1)  
  Knowledge distillation is deployed specifically for time series classification, targeting efficient models for resource-constrained settings. Concrete results plus open artifact make it relevant for those deploying time series learners in production. · 3/5

### Diffusion

- [Dual Latent Memory in Vision-Language-Action Models for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07608) · [github](https://github.com/quhongyu/LaMem-VLA)  
  This work introduces dual latent memory modules to VLA models, pushing robotic manipulation toward long-horizon, temporally complex tasks beyond the Markovian assumption. Open source and trending (40 HF upvotes), it's an actionable advance for embodied, memory-driven agent control. · 3/5

- [ZhengYinan-AIR/Hyper-Diffusion-Planner — The official implementation of "Unleashing the Potential of Diffusion Models for End-to-End Autonomous Driving"](https://github.com/ZhengYinan-AIR/Hyper-Diffusion-Planner)  
  Fully open-source implementation of a diffusion-based planner for self-driving; with 145 GitHub stars, it stands out for real-world applicability beyond academic prototypes. · 3/5

- [soran-ghaderi/torchebm — 🍓 Build and train energy-based and diffusion models in PyTorch ⚡.](https://github.com/soran-ghaderi/torchebm)  
  New library offering a streamlined interface for training energy-based and diffusion models in PyTorch. Trending (97 stars), it's ready for experimentation and deployment. · 3/5

### Dataset

- [ASLP-lab/WSC-Train (HF Dataset)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ASLP-lab/WSC-Train)  
  A new open-source dataset for coreference tasks, highly relevant for language modeling and evaluation. Artifact available (Apache-2.0 license). · 3/5

- [IlyaGusev/habr (HF Dataset)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/IlyaGusev/habr)  
  A large multilingual text-generation dataset (100K<n<1M), spanning Russian and English, for language modeling across modalities. · 2/5

### Other

- [Video2Reaction: Mapping Video to Audience Reaction Distribution in the Wild](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06875v1)  
  Introduces a novel multimodal dataset mapping video segments to distributions of audience reactions, priming content engagement and recommendation system development. Usable artifact provided. · 3/5

- [From Noisy Traces to Root Causes: Structural Trajectory Analysis and Causal Extraction for Agent Optimization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07702v1)  
  Tackles agent trace optimization by extracting causally relevant trajectory structures, enabling actionable reflection mechanisms in long-horizon LLM-based agents. The artifact is available for direct use. · 3/5
