## Main list

### 1. HarnessEval-W: Agentifying the Evaluation of Visual Worlds · 4/5  continuing story (3rd run, traction +3×)  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16859) · [Code](https://github.com/MirroS-Lab/HarnessEval-W) · [Project](https://mirros-lab.github.io/HarnessEval-W) — 223★, 118 HF upvotes  
A benchmark that replaces scalar metrics with an agent judge: the evaluator watches world-model rollouts and flags physics, causality, and state-consistency violations the way a human would. Ships a full harness + leaderboard; the signal is that *evaluation itself* is becoming an agent task.

### 2. Embodied-Navigator: Point, Think, Memorize, and Align for Efficient Navigation · 4/5  continuing story (2nd run, traction +2.9×)  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17512) · [Code](https://github.com/ZJU-OmniAI/Embodied-Omni) · [Project](https://zju-omniai.github.io/Embodied-Navigator/) — 218★, 46 HF upvotes  
TAMP-Nav framework aligns VLM action spaces with their 2D pre-training priors (point → think → memorize → align), adds adaptive memory compression, and beats specialized navigation policies on R2R/RxR. The repo is clean and runnable.

### 3. Wunjo CE: Face Swap, Lip Sync, Control Remove Objects & Text & Background, Restyling, Audio Separator, Clone Voice, Video Generation · 3/5  
[GitHub](https://github.com/wladradchenko/wunjo.wladradchenko.ru) — 1,169★  
A single local web UI bundling every generative-media primitive (InsightFace, GFPGAN, SADTalker, XTTS-v2, Stable Video Diffusion, etc.) with batch processing and Gradio front-end. 1.2k stars in days signals genuine practitioner pull — this is the "ffmpeg for AI media" moment.

### 4. VAKE: Verifiable Activation of Parametric Knowledge via Explicit Priming and Implicit Reasoning · 3/5  
[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18581v1)  
Two-stage RL framework: *Priming* forces the model to surface latent facts as explicit tokens, then *Implicit Reasoning* transfers that activated knowledge to downstream QA. Decouples recall from reasoning — a clean ablation on HotpotQA and 2WikiMultihopQA shows +8–12% EM over end-to-end baselines.
