# AI Radar — 2026-06-15

Major advances this week are anchored by new datasets and benchmarks deepening AI's ability to reason over audio-visual content and medical data with transparency. Release velocity remains high, with a marked emphasis on model evaluation, explainability, agent performance in complex environments, and practical integrations (e.g., continual learning and hardware-aware compression). No major shifts in climbing or cooled items—momentum is with crisp new diagnostic and agent benchmarks.

## What changed

**New today**
- OmniVideo-100K: Full-scale, challenging multi-modal dataset for structured audio-visual reasoning over long contexts.
- ClinHallu: First fine-grained medical MLLM benchmark diagnosing where hallucinations originate in clinical reasoning.
- Several new agent, explainability, and foundation model datasets/benchmarks spanning vision, audio, and scientific applications.
