## Main list

### Benchmark

- [OvisOCR2 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13639)  
A new 0.8B document parsing model capable of end-to-end Markdown conversion from page images, handling text, formulas, tables, and visual regions. Real and synthetic data curation, strong Tier-2 traction (42 HF upvotes), and robust artifact release make this a standout in structured document understanding. · 4/5

- [GSM-Plus-BN: A Perturbation-Based Benchmark for Bangla Mathematical Reasoning in Large Language Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13248v1)  
Introduces a benchmark for mathematical reasoning in Bengali, directly addressing the underrepresentation of low-resource languages (230M+ speakers), opening up equitable AI development for linguistically diverse regions. · 3/5

- [How Far Can Root Cause Analysis Go on Real-World Telemetry Data?](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13548v1)  
Presents the OpenRCA dataset and a critical analysis of root cause detection for production microservices. Multimodal telemetry and practical impacts for reliability engineering; actionable for anyone dealing with complex, large-scale infrastructure. · 3/5

- [Finding the Right Tables and Columns: A Benchmark and Corpus-Adaptive Embeddings for SQL Schema Retrieval](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13311v1)  
Addresses the overlooked challenge of schema retrieval in massive databases, proposing adaptive embeddings and a new benchmark for table/column selection—essential for scalable NL-SQL systems. · 3/5

### Zheng-Chongawesome-Try-On-Models

- [Zheng-Chong/Awesome-Try-On-Models](https://github.com/Zheng-Chong/Awesome-Try-On-Models)  
A well-curated GitHub repo (437 stars) assembling papers, code, and resources for virtual try-on models, useful both for quick research surveys and applied development in fashion tech and e-commerce. · 3/5

### Moe

- [thinkingmachines/Inkling (HF Model)](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling)  
A trending mixture-of-experts (MoE) model for image/text/audio-text conversion. Covers conversational and multimodal scenarios; strong signal in HF trending. · 2/5

### Other

- [DreamSat-Pose: Spacecraft Pose Estimation from Single-View 3D Reconstructions and Learned 2D-3D Feature Matching](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13449v1)  
Introduces a new framework for single-shot 6-DoF pose estimation on unknown spacecraft from a single image, combining 3D reconstructions with learned feature matching. High relevance for robotics and space operations. · 3/5

- [Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Anomaly Detection in Banking Transactions: An Internal Audit Perspective](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13469v1)  
Delivers an XAI framework for banking transaction anomaly detection. Tackles high false-positive rates and compliance transparency—real-world impact in financial and audit domains. · 3/5

- [Unleashing Multimodal Large Language Models for Training-free HOI Detection in the Wild](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13881v1)  
Proposes training-free human-object interaction detection using multimodal LLMs, breaking free of closed-set, dataset-specific constraints. Promising for open-world recognition and robotics. · 3/5

- [GigaWorld-Policy-0.5: A Faster and Stronger WAM Empowered by AutoResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13960) · [project](https://open-gigaai.github.io/giga-world-policy/)  
A notable improvement in robot policy learning: avoids explicit video generation during inference (previous computational bottleneck), enabling faster and more scalable action modeling. 20 HF upvotes; strong boost for efficient robot policy learning. · 2/5
