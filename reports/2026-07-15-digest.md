### Interpretability

- [Explainable-by-Design Audio Deepfake Detection via Wiener-Hopf Linear Prediction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12584v1)  
  Introduces a transparent method for audio deepfake detection based on Wiener-Hopf linear prediction, targeting critical forensic use-cases. Moves beyond black-box approaches, offering interpretability and real artifact availability. · 3/5

- [IQA-T1: Tool-based Visual Evidence Reasoning for Image Quality Assessment](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12375v1)  
  Tackles open-world image quality assessment with a focus on visual evidence reasoning, using tool-driven techniques to enhance interpretability and generalization for IQA tasks. Artifact released. · 3/5

- [Do We Really Need Multimodal Emotion Language Models Larger Than 1B Parameters?](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12787v1)  
  Questions whether scaling MLLMs beyond 1B parameters yields tangible progress in multimodal emotion recognition, providing empirical evidence and interpretability-focused results. · 3/5

### Prism-Mlternary-Bonsai-27B-Gguf

- [prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-27B-gguf (HF Model)](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-27B-gguf)  
  A trending ternary quantized 27B parameter model in gguf format, optimized for resource efficiency (e.g., llama.cpp compatibility), moving toward lightweight deployment. · 2/5

- [GnLOLot/MiniCPM5-1B-Claude-Opus-Fable5-Thinking (HF Model)](https://huggingface.co/GnLOLot/MiniCPM5-1B-Claude-Opus-Fable5-Thinking)  
  Another rising compact text-generation model, built for fast inference and compatible with safetensors and transformers. Useful for experimentation on smaller hardware. · 2/5

### Other

- [roneneldan/TinyStories (HF Dataset)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories)  
  English text-generation dataset (1M-10M samples) trending on HuggingFace. Useful for training compact language models or serving as a foundation for distillation and benchmarking. · 3/5

- [PFAdapter: Hierarchical LoRA Decomposition for Personalized Federated MLLMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12111v1)  
  Proposes a hierarchical LoRA adapter for personalized federated fine-tuning of multimodal large language models, enabling local adaptation while safeguarding data privacy on edge devices. Artifact available. · 3/5

- [Mitigating The Effect of Class Imbalance in Data with Hierarchical and Dependable Structure](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11994v1)  
  Advances approaches for dealing with extreme class imbalance and hierarchical data dependencies, with strong results for cybersecurity vulnerability classification via CWE taxonomy. · 3/5
