## Main list

### Diffusion

**[nunchux-ai/nunchaku — SVDQuant: 4-Bit Diffusion via Low-Rank Outlier Absorption](https://github.com/nunchux-ai/nunchaku) · [code](https://github.com/nunchux-ai/nunchaku) · 3/5**  
ICLR 2025 Spotlight. SVDQuant absorbs activation outliers into low-rank components, enabling 4-bit weight/activation quantization for diffusion models without the quality collapse that plagues naive PTQ. The repo ships ready-to-run inference for SDXL, Flux, and Hunyuan-DiT — 3.9k stars signals this isn't just a paper artifact. If you're deploying diffusion at scale, this is the quantization baseline to beat.

**[teticio/audio-diffusion — Music Generation with Hugging Face Diffusers](https://github.com/teticio/audio-diffusion) · [code](https://github.com/teticio/audio-diffusion) · 3/5**  
Ports the diffusers paradigm to audio: latent diffusion on mel-spectrograms with vocoder decoding, configurable schedulers, and training loops that mirror the image stack. 795 stars in days suggests the "diffusers for audio" abstraction hits a real need. Good starting point if you need controllable music generation without building the plumbing yourself.

**[dealignai/GLM-5.3-CYBERSECURITY-FP8](https://huggingface.co/dealignai/GLM-5.3-CYBERSECURITY-FP8) · 2/5**  
FP8-quantized GLM-5.3 MoE (tags: `glm_moe_dsa`, `abliterated`, `refusal-removed`) positioned for cybersecurity tasks — vulnerability analysis, exploit generation, defensive coding. "Abliterated" + "refusal-removed" means guardrails stripped; treat as a dual-use artifact. Worth evaluating if you're building security tooling and need an open-weight model that doesn't refuse on exploit-like prompts.

**[IFM/K2-Horizon-MoVA-36B-A4B](https://huggingface.co/IFM/K2-Horizon-MoVA-36B-A4B) · 2/5**  
36B parameter MoE (4B active) from the K2-Horizon line. Trending on HF but no accompanying paper or benchmark disclosure yet. Treat as an early artifact — track if IFM publishes evals or the community runs independent benchmarks.

## Insights

- **Diffusion quantization has crossed the "usable in prod" threshold.** SVDQuant (nunchaku) is the third 4-bit diffusion method in two months (after BitDelta and Q-Diffusion) to ship working code with benchmarks — the research-to-deployment lag is compressing.
- **World-model traction is compounding, not spiking.** EchoWM, StreamPI, SolarWM, EditaLive — four distinct architectures all gaining stars/upvotes across 7–11 consecutive runs. This isn't hype cycling; it's multiple groups converging on interactive video generation as the next capability frontier.
- **Security-tuned open weights are appearing without guardrails.** GLM-5.3-CYBERSECURITY-FP8 explicitly strips refusals. Expect more "abliterated" domain models; the safety/competence trade-off is becoming a product decision, not a research one.
