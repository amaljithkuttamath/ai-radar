## Insights

- **Open-source tooling is capturing massive mindshare.** Quivr’s 39.5k stars in days dwarfs typical research-repo traction, signaling pent-up demand for turnkey ingestion/search pipelines.
- **Vision-language models are fragmenting by reasoning “system.”** autotrust ships separate “system-one” (JEV) and “system-two” (GEV) checkpoints — a pattern worth watching for downstream routing.
- **Streaming and long-horizon video are converging on proactive memory.** OneStreamer and the Story-arc cluster (EVO-WAM, HybridCUA, Context LMs) all address retaining evidence before relevance is known.
- **Gameplay benchmarks are maturing into multi-horizon eval.** GameHorizon’s scope (visual, language, planning, control) makes it a stronger proxy for general agent evaluation than narrow arcade suites.

## Action items

- **Read** GameHorizon and OneStreamer papers — both have code and project pages; the former matches your planning/evaluation focus.
- **Try** Quivr locally if you need a self-hosted ingestion + alerting stack; 39k stars means community plugins will appear fast.
- **Track** autotrust’s model family — JEV/GEV split suggests a routing strategy; test both on your VL tasks before committing.
- **Experiment** with next-token-prediction in JS if you need client-side or edge inference without Python overhead.
