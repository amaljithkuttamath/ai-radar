# AI Radar — 2026-06-30

The past week saw a distinct shift toward practical agent benchmarks and streaming applications. Standout: advances in agentic reasoning and a method for achieving trillion-parameter performance with a more efficient agent model. Benchmarks for long-horizon, computer-use, and temporal reasoning confirm there's strong traction in pushing agents beyond toy settings. The window was widened to 7 days to surface clear signal.

## Main list

### Agents

**[Scaling the Horizon, Not the Parameters: Reaching Trillion-Parameter Performance with a 35B Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30616) · [code](https://github.com/InternScience/Agents-A1) · [project](https://internscience.github.io/Agents-A1/) · 59 HF upvotes · 4/5**
A 35B Mixture-of-Experts model achieves trillion-parameter-level performance—without actually scaling to a trillion parameters—by extending the "agent horizon". This is a concrete step toward practical ultra-large systems, with the code publicly released.

**[OSWorld2.0: Benchmarking Computer Use Agents on Long-Horizon Real-World Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29537) · [code](https://github.com/xlang-ai/OSWorld-V2) · [project](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/) · 8 HF upvotes · 3/5**
OSWorld 2.0 provides a benchmark for computer-use agents tackling realistic, complex, and long-horizon desktop tasks. Covers 108 scenarios; usable artifact for evaluating agentic models.

**[TUA-Bench: A Benchmark for General-Purpose Terminal-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28480) · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/TUA-Bench) · 38 HF upvotes · 3/5**
A new benchmark for LLM agents operating in terminal environments beyond code, expanding the range and realism of what agents must handle.

### Benchmark

**[Video-MME-Logical: A Controlled Diagnostic Benchmark for Video Temporal-Logical Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27828) · [code](https://github.com/Mrakas/video-mme-logical) · [project](https://mrakas.github.io/video-mme-logical) · 23 HF upvotes · 3/5**
Tests whether multimodal LLMs can reason about dynamic video, not just recognize objects. The benchmark enables diagnostic evaluation of temporal-logical reasoning—crucial for video agent tasks.

**[Bridging VideoQA and Video-Guided Agentic Tasks via Generalized Keyframe Extraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29445) · [code](https://github.com/VG-GUI-TASKER/VG-GUI-TASKER) · [project](https://vg-gui-tasker.github.io/) · 15 HF upvotes · 3/5**
Connects VideoQA and agentic task benchmarks by extracting keyframes, helping assess how video understanding generalizes to agentic tasks.

**[MemoBench: Benchmarking World Modeling in Dynamically Changing Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27537) · [code](https://github.com/MemoBench-Team/MemoBench) · [project](https://memobench-team.github.io/) · 3 HF upvotes · 3/5**
Challenges generation models to maintain world consistency in settings where objects leave and return, advancing benchmarks for memory and dynamic world modeling.

### Other

**[Agentic Abstention: Do Agents Know When to Stop Instead of Act?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28733) · [project](https://lhannnn.github.io/agentic-abstention) · 106 HF upvotes · 4/5**
Addresses a central question in agent design: can LLM agents reliably abstain from acting when a goal is ill-specified or unreachable? The method and accompanying artifact surface agentic "refusal" as a measurable skill.

**[LiveEdit: Towards Real-Time Diffusion-Based Streaming Video Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26740) · [code](https://github.com/cp-cp/LiveEdit) · 63 HF upvotes · 4/5**
Introduces a real-time, diffusion-based streaming video editor that maintains spatial stability and achieves low latency for practical deployment, with open code.

**[Beyond IID: How General Are Tabular Foundation Models, Really?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30410) · [project](https://tabarena.ai/) · 33 HF upvotes · 4/5**
Evaluates foundation models for tabular data across genuinely diverse real-world datasets, probing their generalization limits. Useful for practitioners deploying models on tabular tasks.

**[stas00/ml-engineering — Machine Learning Engineering Open Book](https://github.com/stas00/ml-engineering) · 18,193 GitHub stars · 3/5**
An open-source book and artifact compiling ML engineering best practices. High traction; likely useful reference for practitioners.

## Insights

- Benchmarks are trending toward realism—long-horizon tasks, real-world environments, and memory—rather than synthetic toy problems.
- Strong upvotes and star counts concentrate around practical agent benchmarks and engineering artifacts, not theory.
- Agentic reasoning (knowing when not to act) is becoming a distinct measurable capability, not just an emergent property.
- Video and multimodal benchmarks are converging with agentic task evaluation; future benchmarks will merge these dimensions.
