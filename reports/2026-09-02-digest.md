### Main list

#### Evaluation
Four benchmarks and an open MoE report arrived together, each from a strong group with code or weights attached. They don't just measure — they define new tasks.

- **[CopyShield: A Cross-Level Benchmark of Copyright Defenses in LLMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01161v1)** — Controlled comparison of three defense layers (contrastive decoding, DPO, activation intervention) on LLaMA-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B over five public-domain books. Same protocol, literal + non-literal leakage measured. · 3/5
- **[Who Judges the Judges? A Chinese Safety QA Benchmark](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01210v1)** — C-SafeQA: 538 base queries expanded to 8,877 adversarial variants, answered by four deployed LLMs, 37,660 labeled records. Policy-grounded response-level evaluation, not just query risk. · 3/5
- **[CATeye: Coupled Attribute-Topology Invariance Learning for Voucher Abuse Detection](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01425v1)** — Fraud detection benchmark where attribute and topology shifts are coupled; the method learns invariance across both. Real e-commerce distribution-shift problem, not a toy split. · 3/5
- **[Instella-MoE Technical Report](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00791v1)** — Fully open 16B-total/2.8B-active MoE trained from scratch on AMD MI300X/MI325X. Gated MLA, FarSkip-Collective connectivity, multi-stage pipeline (pre/mid/long-context). Weights + training recipe released. · 3/5

#### Continued heavy hitters
- **[ASI-Bench: At the Dawn of Artificial Superintelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17271)** — 12th run, still climbing (277 stars, 64 HF upvotes). Benchmarks ASI as "exploring the unknown, creating new knowledge, verifiable results" — not compressing known corpus. Continuing story, not new arrival. · 4/5
- **[Embodied-Navigator: Point, Think, Memorize, and Align for Efficient Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17512)** — 12th run, 235 stars, 51 HF upvotes. TAMP-Nav framework aligns VLM 2D priors with navigation action spaces; unified reasoning + memory. Continuing story. · 4/5

#### Agentic & systems
- **[H2Table: Hierarchical Hypergraph-Enhanced LLMs for Complex Table Reasoning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01216v1)** — Represents tables as nested hypergraphs (headers as hyperedges, cells as nodes) with a tailored encoder. Beats linearization baselines on complex reasoning splits. · 3/5
- **[Some Emotions Run Deeper: Layer-wise Probing and Causal Intervention in LLMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01279v1)** — Probes 8 open-weight LLMs (1B–9B, Llama/Qwen/Granite) across three corpora (Twitter, Reddit, autobiographical). Emotion depth depends on both model layer and text explicitness; causal interventions confirm. · 3/5
- **[ISTA-DASLab/Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/ISTA-DASLab/Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-GGUF)** — Mixed-precision GSQ quantization with RCO (rotation-aware calibration optimization) from IST Austria DASLab. Artifact ready for local inference. · 3/5
- **[ItzCrazyKns/Vane](https://github.com/ItzCrazyKns/Vane)** — AI-powered answering engine, TypeScript, 36.6k stars. Clean repo, active community. More product than paper, but traction is undeniable. · 3/5

### Insights
- **Evaluation is fragmenting by threat model.** Copyright (CopyShield), Chinese safety policy (C-SafeQA), e-commerce fraud (CATeye), and ASI capabilities (ASI-Bench) each demand a bespoke protocol — no single "safety benchmark" suffices.
- **Open MoE is becoming a system co-design target.** Instella-MoE's Gated MLA + FarSkip-Collective + AMD MI300X stack shows the artifact is now the *training system*, not just weights.
- **Long-horizon agents are converging on memory + evolution.** JIT-Agent, Agentic ESOpt, Recursive Experiential-Working Memory, and Embodied-Navigator all treat the agent as a continually self-modifying system, not a static policy.
- **Quantization is specializing by hardware.** ISTA-DASLab's GSQ-RCO targets mixed-precision on specific GPU architectures; the "one quantization recipe fits all" era is ending.
