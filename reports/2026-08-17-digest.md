## Main list

### 1. AutoDesign: Meta-Harness Optimization for Long-Horizon Agentic Design · 4/5  
**[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13560) · [code](https://github.com/Yaxin9Luo/AutoDesign) · [project](https://autodesign.designanything.ai/)** — A framework that treats multimodal content production as a long-horizon agentic task, letting a model-harness system accumulate reusable experience through empirical exploration and recursively self-improve. 68 GitHub stars, 47 HF upvotes. **Continuing story** (3rd run, traction still climbing).

### 2. PlayWorld: Benchmarking World Models with Agent Players over Long-Horizon Objectives · 4/5  
**[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13552) · [code](https://github.com/kxding/PlayWorld) · [project](https://kxding.github.io/project/PlayWorld/)** — First benchmark that evaluates video world models by having *agent players* pursue long-horizon objectives inside the simulated world, rather than static metrics. 54 stars, 43 HF upvotes. **Continuing story** (3rd run, strong traction growth).

### 3. fallenshock/FlowEdit — Inversion-Free Text-Based Editing Using Pre-Trained Flow Models · 4/5  
**[code](https://github.com/fallenshock/FlowEdit)** — Official implementation of the FlowEdit paper: edits images via text prompts on pre-trained flow models *without* the costly DDIM inversion step required by diffusion editors. 1,013 GitHub stars in days — the clearest practitioner signal this window.

### 4. FareedKhan-dev/train-llm-from-scratch — End-to-End LLM Training Pipeline · 3/5  
**[code](https://github.com/FareedKhan-dev/train-llm-from-scratch)** — A readable, runnable walkthrough from data download through tokenization, training, and generation. 9,144 stars — broad educational pull, not a research advance.

### 5. JonathanColetti/Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored-GGUF · 3/5  
**[model](https://huggingface.co/JonathanColetti/Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored-GGUF)** — Uncensored Qwen 3.8 27B in GGUF with MTP and speculative-decoding tags; drop-in for llama.cpp. Trending on HF Models — useful if you need an open-weight 27B without alignment filters.

## Insights

- **Agentic harnesses are becoming a subfield.** AutoDesign, PlayWorld, Evo-Bench, and A²E all orbit the same idea: the *harness* around a model is where long-horizon capability lives, and that harness can itself be optimized, benchmarked, and audited.  
- **Flow models are stealing diffusion's editing thunder.** FlowEdit's inversion-free approach + 1K stars in days suggests practitioners are ready to swap toolchains when the ergonomics win is clear.  
- **Benchmark datasets are shipping as first-class HF artifacts.** HLE and fable-5-premium show the platform becoming the default release surface for evals — versioned, licensed, parquet-native.  
- **Uncensored model variants remain a steady demand signal.** The Qwen 27B GGUF trend reflects a persistent practitioner need: capable open weights without refusal behavior, quantized for local inference.
