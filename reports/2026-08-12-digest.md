## Main list

### Agentic
**[tsiendragon/qwen-image-finetune](https://github.com/tsiendragon/qwen-image-finetune) · 4/5**  
A practical fine-tuning toolkit for Qwen-Image that hit 252 GitHub stars in its first 48h. Covers LoRA, full fine-tuning, and inference scripts — the kind of repo that moves a model from "available" to "usable in production" for teams building custom image pipelines. [GitHub](https://github.com/tsiendragon/qwen-image-finetune)

**[Agentic Instruction Data Selection: Let DataMaster Interpret Your Intent](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10579v1) · 2/5**  
Replaces manual heuristic data-selection rules with an agent (DataMaster) that reads your intent and composes selection strategies automatically. Early results show it matches or beats hand-crafted pipelines across diverse instruction datasets. [Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10579v1)

### Meta-Models
**[meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B-GGUF) · 3/5**  
Quantized GGUF release of the 30B Muse-Glimmer multimodal model (image-text-to-text). Runs on consumer GPUs; the base model draws from arXiv:2504.13181 and 2602.06036. A concrete artifact for teams needing strong vision-language reasoning locally. [HF Model](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B-GGUF) · [Base Model](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B)

**[Lightricks/LTX-2.5](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5) · 2/5**  
New video diffusion model supporting text-to-video, image-to-video, video-to-video, and audio-to-video. Single-file diffusion weights; early community reports suggest strong temporal coherence at 24fps. [HF Model](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5)

### Cost & Efficiency
**[DEFT: Data-Efficient Frequency-domain Top-k Sampling for Spatiotemporal Dynamical Systems](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11019v1) · 3/5**  
Identifies dominant Fourier modes of a physical system and varies their amplitudes/phases to generate physically consistent training samples — cuts data requirements for PDE surrogate modeling by targeting the spectral basis that matters. Code released. [Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11019v1)

**[Retrieval-Augmented Vision Foundation Models for Robust Leukemia Classification](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10657v1) · 3/5**  
Two-stage pipeline (binary detection → subtype classification) using a pretrained vision foundation model with retrieval across 122K single-cell images. Handles staining, illumination, and site protocol shifts that break single-dataset models. Clinical-grade robustness focus. [Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10657v1)

### Other
**[SKILLER: Language-Level RL for Reusable Skill Extraction in Small LMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10538v1) · 3/5**  
Extracts reusable "skills" (standardized procedural knowledge) via RL on small open models, enabling cheap agent harness deployment. Targets the Codex/OpenClaw cost problem by making skill execution viable on consumer GPUs. [Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10538v1)
