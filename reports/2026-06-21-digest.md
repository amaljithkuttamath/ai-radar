## Main list

- [openai/gsm8k (HF Dataset)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k)  
  OpenAI’s GSM8K — the gold-standard dataset for grade school math reasoning — is now officially on Hugging Face, making it far easier for practitioners to run standardized evaluations, finetune, or baseline math-capable LLMs. It’s an essential benchmark artifact, widely used for measuring math proficiency of language models, and its official listing here indicates broad traction and practical utility. · 4/5

- [Mia-AiLab/Qwable-3.6-27b (HF Model)](https://huggingface.co/Mia-AiLab/Qwable-3.6-27b)  
  Qwable-3.6-27b, a new large-scale LLM leveraging the Qwen3 architecture, is trending on Hugging Face. While details are sparse, its presence suggests active experimentation with next-gen transformer variants. Worth watching for updates and benchmarks. · 2/5
