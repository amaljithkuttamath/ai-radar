## Main list

### Video World Models
**SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models** · 4/5  
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886) · [Code](https://github.com/Junchao-cs/SolarWM) · [Project](https://junchao-cs.github.io/SolarWM-Web/)  
*Continuing story — 7th run, traction 3.4× since Sep 3 (557 GH stars, 147 HF upvotes).* SolarWM tackles the core headache of video world models: heterogeneous data (different temporal scales, camera geometries, caption styles) and incompatible generator backbones. The release spans curated datasets, training recipes across multiple architectures, and long-horizon inference tooling — a full stack, not a model drop. For teams building simulators, robotics policies, or generative video pipelines, this is the most complete open starting point available.

**apple-aiml-research/ml-mdm — Train high-quality text-to-image diffusion models in a data & compute efficient manner** · 3/5  
[Repo](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-mdm) (516 stars)  
Apple's entry into efficient diffusion training: a modular codebase for masked diffusion models (MDM) that cuts compute without quality loss. The repo includes configs, training scripts, and pretrained checkpoints. Practical for teams who need diffusion at smaller scale — research labs, on-device inference, or anyone priced out of full Stable Diffusion training runs.

### New Model Releases
**Edge0/Edge0-35B-A3B-preview** · 3/5  
[Model](https://huggingface.co/Edge0/Edge0-35B-A3B-preview)  
A 35B MoE (3B active) built on Qwen3.5 with a pre-router for edge inference — tags include `mlx`, `safetensors`, `prerouter`. The architecture targets efficient local deployment; weights are open. Worth a look if you're evaluating small MoEs for on-device or edge serving, though benchmarks are thin so far.

**openbmb/MiniCPM5-2B-GGUF** · 2/5  
[Model](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM5-2B-GGUF)  
MiniCPM's latest 2B variant in GGUF format — multimodal, Transformers-compatible, ready for llama.cpp. The MiniCPM line has a track record of punching above its weight class; this is the newest checkpoint. Try it if you need a tiny multimodal model that runs locally.

### Protein & Benchmark Infrastructure
**microsoft/foldingdiff — Diffusion models of protein structure; trigonometry and attention are all you need!** · 4/5  
[Repo](https://github.com/microsoft/foldingdiff) (567 stars) · *2nd run, traction flat*  
Microsoft's diffusion-based protein structure generator — SE(3)-equivariant, trained on CATH, generates valid folds without structure prediction as an intermediate step. The approach is distinct from AlphaFold/ESMFold: it's a generative model over backbone frames, not a predictor. Active repo, clean code, useful for protein design workflows.

**gaia-benchmark/GAIA (HF Dataset)** · 4/5  
[Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gaia-benchmark/GAIA) · *2nd run, traction flat*  
The GAIA benchmark dataset (multi-modal: audio, document, image) now trending on HF. If you're evaluating agent tool-use or multimodal reasoning, this is the canonical test set — 466 questions across three difficulty levels, designed to resist memorization.

## Insights
- **Open artifacts beat papers for traction.** Four of the seven main-list items are code repos or model weights; the three papers (SolarWM, EarlyEval, GAIA) all ship usable artifacts alongside the PDF. Pure papers without code/weights are notably absent from the top tier.
- **Video world models are consolidating around open data + multi-backbone training.** SolarWM and Apple's ml-mdm both address the "heterogeneous data, heterogeneous architectures" problem from opposite angles (video vs. image), suggesting this is the current bottleneck for the field.
- **Small MoEs are the new deployment target.** Edge0's 35B/3B MoE with a pre-router and MiniCPM5-2B both optimize for edge/local inference — the architecture trend is shifting from "how big" to "how efficiently routed."
