## Main list

**[XingChen-AGI/TeleOCR](https://huggingface.co/XingChen-AGI/TeleOCR) · 3/5**  
A Qwen2.5-VL–based OCR model released on Hugging Face, targeting document parsing and image-text-to-text tasks. Backed by the XingChen-AGI group (frontier authorship) and showing strong Tier-2 traction signals. Weights and tokenizer are available immediately via `transformers`/`safetensors`.

**[inclusionAI/Ming-Image-0.1-Design](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ming-Image-0.1-Design) · 2/5**  
A text-to-image diffusion model fine-tuned for graphic-design workflows, distributed through Hugging Face with `diffusers`/`safetensors` support. Trending on HF with solid Tier-2 traction; the design-domain specialization is the differentiator versus general-purpose generators.

## Insights

- **HF model hub is the new launchpad** — both top new items shipped as ready-to-run HF models with `transformers`/`diffusers` integration, bypassing traditional paper-first cycles.
- **Design and document understanding are attracting specialized models** — Ming-Image (design) and TeleOCR (documents) suggest a trend toward domain-narrowed multimodal releases rather than monolithic "omni" models.
- **Long-horizon video control remains a compounding theme** — LynnReal-Omni, the climbing memory paper, and multiple story-arc items all attack temporal coherence from different angles (agentic control, memory mechanisms, diffusion RL).
- **Recursive self-improvement agents dominate the story arcs** — 6 of 10 climbing arcs involve agents that improve their own training data or policies (EvoOntology, RSIAgent, ScienceBuddy, onPanda, Harness-Zero, privileged-info distillation).
